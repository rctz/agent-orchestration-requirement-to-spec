# plain-english

Every field you author is read by an implementer who will be judged by it.

- Short sentences. Present tense. Active voice.
- Name the actor and the action: "The CLI prints the converted value with
  two decimals" — not "the value is output appropriately".
- Banned words (they survive review as P2 polish findings): robust,
  user-friendly, seamless, intuitive, state-of-the-art, leverage,
  cutting-edge, comprehensive, modern, powerful.
- Numbers over adjectives: "under 200 ms at 50 concurrent users" not
  "fast".
- No apologies, no hedging, no marketing. If a sentence can be deleted
  without losing a fact, delete it.
- Artifacts are English regardless of the user's language; questions and
  user-facing text follow the job's language.
